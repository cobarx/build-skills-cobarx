# 0006. Industry terms over our own

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided:** 2026-09-20 (#1) · **Recorded:** 2026-09-22 · Claude Opus 5.5
- **Affects:** `glossary` (planned), and the vocabulary of every skill

## Context

A skill library names a lot of things: practices, roles, kinds of document. Each name is either an
established term or one we invent.

## Options

- **Coin our own vocabulary.**
- **Adopt the industry term**, coining only where none exists.

## Decision

"Industry terms over our own. Coinage is permitted for constructs local to this repo, and is
recorded as ours." (CLAUDE.md, #1.)

The reasoning was not written down at the time; the convention is stated without it.

## Consequences

- Choices follow it: Brooks's essential and accidental over a coinage (0005), MADR over a
  home-grown record format (0001).
- Registering a term, industry or coined, is planned as `glossary` (#20).
- Known coinages: `docs/essays/` (`docs/context/open-questions.md`) and `durable-context` (flagged
  in review of #18). Until `glossary` exists they have no register.
