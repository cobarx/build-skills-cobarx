# 0005. Brooks's essential and accidental complexity, not a coinage

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided:** 2026-09-20 (#1) · **Recorded:** 2026-09-22 · Claude Opus 5.5
- **Affects:** `simplicity`

## Context

`simplicity` needs to separate complexity that must be accepted from complexity that should be
removed.

## Options

- **Brooks's essential and accidental complexity**: Fred Brooks, "No Silver Bullet: Essence and
  Accidents of Software Engineering" (1986; IEEE Computer 20(4), 1987).
- **A spend/save/earn coinage.** Its details were not recorded.

## Decision

Brooks. The rule reads: "Complexity essential to the problem must be paid for; complexity
introduced by the solution is waste."

The weighing was not written down at the time; #1 notes it "exists only in conversation". It is the
choice 0006 prescribes: an established industry term over one of our own.

## Consequences

- Readers can look the distinction up; nothing about it needs defining in the skill.
